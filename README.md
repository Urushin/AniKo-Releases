## Why AniKo

| | |
|---|---|
| 📚 **A polished reader** | PDF, CBZ, unprotected EPUB, images. Automatic resume, vertical or page-by-page reading. |
| 🎬 **A complete video player** | Your own files and servers, with resume and subtitles. |
| 📈 **Reliable tracking** | AniList and MyAnimeList sync, release calendar. |
| 🖥️ **Your servers** | Komga, Kavita, Suwayomi, Aniyomi-compatible. |
| 🧩 **Your own modules** | Add modules yourself, switched on one by one, only after your consent. |
| 🔒 **Private by default** | No AniKo account. Everything stays on your device. |

> AniKo does not host, stream or recommend any content. You use your own files, servers and modules, under your own responsibility.

## Install

You need [AltStore](https://altstore.io) or [SideStore](https://sidestore.io) already installed on your iPhone.

1. On your iPhone, tap one of the **Add to…** buttons at the top of this page.
2. Or add the source by hand: *Sources* → **+** → paste

   ```
   https://mangaanimezone.pages.dev/altstore/apps.json
   ```
3. Open **AniKo** in the source, tap **Free**, then **Install**.
4. If the app does not open: *Settings → General → VPN & Device Management* → trust your profile.

Or download the **IPA** directly from the [latest release](https://github.com/Urushin/AniKo-Releases/releases/latest).

> With a free Apple account the app must be refreshed every 7 days. AltStore and SideStore do it automatically.

